Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Fine Print Money publishes educational videos. This policy covers this website and the private publishing tools we use to post our own videos to our own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts.

What we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and it does not collect personal information. Our publishing tools connect only to accounts we own. Through each platform's official API, they access only what's needed to upload our videos and read performance statistics for our own content. We do not collect, store, sell or share data about other users.

Google and YouTube data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at Google security settings.

Data storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored privately on our own systems and are never published. To ask about or request deletion of any data, email fineprintmoney99@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the update on this page with a new date.
